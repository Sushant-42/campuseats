# CampusEats — Project Brief

## What

CampusEats is a campus food delivery system. Students can log in, manage their profile and delivery addresses, browse campus restaurants and menus, add items to a cart, place orders, make payments, and track their food delivery. The system also provides notifications as the order progresses.

## Who

The main users of the system are:

- Students
- Restaurants
- Riders/delivery personnel
- System services responsible for payments and notifications

## Nouns

The main nouns/entities in the system are:

- Student
- User

- Restaurant
- Menu

- Cart
- Order
- Payment
- Rider
- Delivery
- Notification
